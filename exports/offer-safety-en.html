Introduction & Overview

An integrated vision for occupational-safety management

We are pleased to present to the Occupational Health & Safety department of Alex Apparels a specialized module within the Zacode ERP system, designed to fully manage the safety ecosystem inside garment factories — from logging incidents and assessing risks, to managing protective equipment, training, emergencies and compliance with local and international standards.

The module operates in connection with the rest of the factory's departments (production, warehouses, human resources), linking every worker, every production line and every machine to its own safety record, and transforming safety procedures from scattered paperwork into a unified, measurable and real-time digital system.

Safety Operations Management

From detecting the hazard to closing the action
🚨

Incidents, Injuries & Reports

  • Log incidents, injuries and near-misses (Near-Miss)
  • Classify each incident by type, severity and location
  • Investigate and analyze the root cause of each incident
  • Track lost work days and compensation
⚠️

Risk Assessment & Hazard Register

  • Hazard register per department, line and machine
  • Risk-level scoring (likelihood × impact)
  • Risk-control and mitigation plans to an acceptable level
  • Periodic review and continuous register updates
🦺

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Issue equipment per worker by size and task
  • Track equipment validity and replacement dates
  • Link issuance to inventory and cost
  • PPE-compliance reports
🔍

Inspections & Work Permits

  • Periodic inspection rounds with checklists
  • Permit to Work for hazardous tasks
  • Capture and document violations with photos
  • Automatic scheduling of periodic inspections
🛠️

Corrective & Preventive Actions (CAPA)

  • Open a corrective action for every violation or incident
  • Assign an owner and due date to each action
  • Track action status through to final closure
  • Automatic alerts for overdue actions
⚙️

Machine Safety Maintenance

  • A safety card for every machine and piece of equipment
  • Scheduling of periodic preventive maintenance
  • Link breakdowns to safety hazards
  • Prevent operation of unsafe machines

Training, Emergency & Occupational Health

Full readiness and continuous worker protection
🎓

Training & Certifications

  • A safety-training record for each worker
  • Track certifications and their renewal dates
  • Alerts before certifications expire
  • Assess training comprehension
🔥

Emergency, Evacuation & Fire

  • Evacuation plans and assembly-point definition
  • Schedule periodic fire and evacuation drills
  • Track firefighting equipment and its validity
  • Document drill results and lessons learned
🧪

Chemical Management (MSDS)

  • Register of chemicals used (dyeing / printing)
  • Safety data sheets (MSDS) for each material
  • Safe storage and handling requirements
  • Track stock and expiry dates
🩺

Occupational Health & Medical Exams

  • Record of periodic medical examinations for workers
  • Track occupational illnesses and special cases
  • Link the work environment to health hazards
  • Alerts for periodic-examination dates
✦

Compliance & International Standards

Full adherence to laws and standards
📋 Local Compliance — Egyptian Labor Law
  • Adherence to occupational health & safety requirements
  • Work-environment requirements and safe working hours
  • Safety records required by regulatory authorities
  • Reports ready for government inspection
🌐 International Standards — ISO 45001
  • Build a safety-management system per ISO 45001
  • The continuous-improvement cycle (Plan-Do-Check-Act)
  • Structured documentation of procedures and policies
  • Full readiness for audit and accreditation requirements
