Introduction

An integrated vision for factory operations

To the Esteemed Chairman of the Board of Alex Apparels, we are pleased to present the integrated Zacode ERP system for managing and operating garment & textile factories serving local and export markets — designed to connect all factory departments in a single platform that ensures full control, raises operational efficiency, reduces costs, and improves the quality of decision-making.

The system provides comprehensive management of every operational and administrative process inside the factory — from raw-material procurement through to delivering the finished product to customers inside Egypt and abroad, including the Gulf and other export markets.

Tax Compliance & E-Invoicing

Ready for both the Egyptian and Gulf markets

The system supports e-invoicing requirements across more than one market, serving local sales and export simultaneously.

🇪🇬 Egyptian Market — Electronic Invoicing (ETA)
  • Issue e-invoices in the Egyptian Tax Authority format
  • Item codes (GS1 / EGS) and tax linkage
  • Track submission and approval status
  • Value-added tax (VAT) reports
🇸🇦 Gulf Markets — ZATCA E-Invoicing
  • UBL 2.1 invoices and approved QR (TLV) code
  • CSR / CSID certificate management
  • Readiness for export and sales within Gulf states
  • Bilingual (Arabic/English) invoice templates
