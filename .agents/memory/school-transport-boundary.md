---
name: School transport boundary
description: Ownership, opt-in, and scope boundaries for school transportation.
---

School-owned buses reference the school's existing fixed assets. Rented buses reference the existing supplier and lease details; rental payments stay in the existing supplier-invoice and accounting flows, with no parallel payment or ledger path.

Transportation is optional per student. An active assignment is the opt-in; creating or promoting a school enrollment must not automatically assign a bus.

Regular academic-year transportation and future summer remedial classes are separate scopes. Do not represent summer sessions as regular-year transport assignments.

**Why:** The user specified that school-owned buses are assets, external buses are rented, transportation is optional, and summer remedial classes are future scope.

**How to apply:** Preserve these boundaries when extending school transportation, supplier billing, enrollment, or summer-program features.
