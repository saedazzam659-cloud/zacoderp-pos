---
name: School fee accounting boundary
description: Preserve the K–12 module as school operations while keeping fee invoicing and settlement in the existing ERP.
---

School fee records are operational school data and must link to the existing sales-invoice workflow. Do not create a separate payment, collection, ledger, or posting path for school fees. Existing ERP invoicing, receipt/collection, and accounting flows remain authoritative.

**Why:** The school module is an addition to the ERP, not a second accounting system; duplicating financial flows risks inconsistent balances and fiscal documents.

**How to apply:** Keep school fee charges linked to sales invoices and route all invoice creation, settlement, and posting through existing ERP screens and services.

For collection UX, recommend the multi-receipt voucher as the prominent path for school-linked sales invoices, but do not enforce it as the only path. Keep standard receipt and invoice quick-payment flows available.

**Why:** The user chose guided use of multi-receipt rather than a hard restriction, preserving ordinary one-invoice payments and existing ERP workflows.

**How to apply:** Provide a visible shortcut from school fee management to multi-receipt creation; do not block other settlement routes or create a separate school payment flow.
