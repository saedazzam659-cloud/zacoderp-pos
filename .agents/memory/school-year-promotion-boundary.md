---
name: School-year promotion boundary
description: Preserve one student identity across school years while maintaining a separate, immutable enrollment record for each year.
---

Keep one student profile across the full academic history. Annual promotion creates a new enrollment for the destination year and never edits or replaces prior-year enrollments. Review class destinations before promotion, skip students already enrolled in the destination year, and enforce company isolation and school permissions. Optional copied fee or discount values are enrollment starting values only; promotion must not create fee charges or invoices.

**Why:** Past classes, attendance, and results are part of the student's academic record, while financial documents must remain in the ERP's existing billing flows.

**How to apply:** For future school-year transitions, add a new year-scoped enrollment, preserve historical rows, verify destination classes and duplicate enrollment state, and keep fee/invoice creation outside the promotion operation.
