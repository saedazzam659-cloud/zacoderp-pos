---
name: Fiscal-year policy boundary
description: Company-specific fiscal-year settings, annual close, and preservation of existing fiscal-year records.
---

The first fiscal year for a newly activated company starts on its effective subscription/activation date and ends on December 31 of that year; do not seed periods before activation. Older operations are added only through an explicit migration/import. A custom company cycle repeats annually with the same duration. Monthly close must not create a fiscal year. Final annual close remains an authorized approval; reuse an exact, open successor instead of creating another year, and derive carry-forward from posted history without a duplicate opening journal. If posted opening entries already exist in that successor, compare them by account and block close with visible differences rather than editing entries or posting an automatic adjustment. When explicitly selected for a prior year with an exact open successor, allow closing only the prior year without creating, reconciling, or changing anything in the successor; normal current-year rollover stays unchanged. Existing and historical year records are not rewritten when policy changes, and manual creation remains available.

Annual close must check each item/warehouse's stock-ledger quantity through that fiscal year's end date and block both year-level close steps while any balance is negative. The optional purchase shortcut opens a prefilled draft only; its date must reflect the actual purchase because later purchases cannot repair a historical year-end balance.

**Why:** Companies activate in different months, so automatically creating earlier periods implies an operating history the company may not have. Changing historical periods can also corrupt period assignments and accounting evidence. Some companies finalize a prior year after the next year is already in use and require the next year to remain exactly as it is.

**How to apply:** Pass the effective activation date through every new-company provisioning path. Treat configured cycles as company-scoped templates after the initial stub year. In the normal rollover flow, reuse the exact open successor and reconcile any posted opening journal against prior-year closing balances; require manual resolution for differences. Only skip that successor reconciliation when the user explicitly chooses prior-year-only close, and never write to the successor in that path. Keep manual creation available for migration and existing-year setup. For stock checks, sum company/item/warehouse movements through the selected year-end date, recheck server-side at close, and keep purchase drafts unposted until the user verifies supplier and actual date.
