---
name: Fiscal-year policy boundary
description: Company-specific fiscal-year settings, annual close, and preservation of existing fiscal-year records.
---

The Gregorian year is the default for new companies, not a universal requirement. A custom company cycle repeats annually with the same duration. Monthly close must not create a fiscal year. Final annual close remains an authorized approval; the successor uses the closed year's actual dates and posted ledger history, without a duplicate opening journal. Existing and historical year records are not rewritten when policy changes, and manual creation remains available for migrations.

**Why:** Fiscal-year boundaries vary by company, while changing historical periods can corrupt period assignments and accounting evidence.

**How to apply:** Treat the configured cycle as a company-scoped template. Before rollover, ensure the actual fiscal-year record matches it; use explicit, audited workflows for any change to an existing period.
