---
name: Public module screenshots
description: Product boundary for screenshots shown in the public ERP module gallery.
---

The login-page module gallery is a public marketing surface managed by SuperAdmin. Treat every uploaded screenshot as public content; use sanitized demo data and do not publish screenshots containing company or customer information without review.

**Why:** Public visitors need real product views, but operational screens may expose tenant data.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing gallery content, preserve SuperAdmin control and verify screenshots are appropriate for public display.
